## Deliberate skips

The course skips only what Lang himself sends to later chapters. Where Lang names the later chapter, the lesson gives the same one-line note.

| Topic | Where Lang mentions it | Why it is skipped here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Simultaneous equations as intersecting lines | Ch 2 §1 | Lang refers to coordinates, a later chapter |
| Intersections of lines with circles and conics | Ch 4 | Lang refers to a later chapter |
| Square roots of negative numbers | Ch 4 | Lang refers to the chapter on complex numbers |
| Irrational exponents, such as 2 to the power √2 | Ch 3 §3 | Lang postpones them to a more advanced course |
| A formal proof that *a*ⁿ < *b*ⁿ for all *n* | Ch 3 §4 | Lang refers to induction, a later chapter |
| Why each real number exists (completeness) | Ch 3 §3 | Lang assumes existence and postpones the proof |
